# Project Ideas Collection

## ChanceTrade

A probability of an event happening based share market, where people buy
and trade the share (priced the same as the probability...range
00--100%)...applicable to sports and other events...Purely skill based.

## Gigstar

A gig application, where people can put their small jobs and
requirements, along with their payment and others can find it and accept
the gigs. The gig workers and job givers will have their own profile and
rating.

## Homedel

A delivery app for the hotels and restaurants, bypassing the
conventional food delivery apps. Has all the logistics tech embedded.
Can work closely with Gigstar for constant onboarding.

## Hommunity

Apartment / Society management app with app-based security.

## Tenman App

Tenant management app where each and every transaction is monitored and
has a double approval system to stop issues happening later.

## Hourloom

Bespoke watch company with an additional creator marketplace where
designers can sell or showcase their watch designs.

## Judge

Zero Court; where an AI-powered judge is provided the full conditions
and gives a judgement, includes appeals up to 10 times with new facts or
corrections. Done by policemen at police stations during FIR.

## Knockster

Visitor management app for industries, tech parks, hospitals, schools,
high-security areas, etc. Has 4-layer security check, where clients can
opt from 1 to 4 depending on their needs.

## Lawyer

AI-powered FriendInLaw, where users upload their issues and receive
legal strategies. Intended to be a law first responder.

## Mapogram

-   **Mapogram Social** -- Social media on a map, ending doomscrolling.
-   **SalesOnMap** -- View all potential customers on a map dashboard.
-   **OpsOnMap** -- View all staff and issues on a map dashboard.
-   **AssetsOnMap** -- View all assets on a map and monitor condition.
-   **ComplaintsOnMap** -- Customer service on a map with geolocated
    complaints.

## Newsome News Group

-   **Multi Perspective News** -- News presented from all involved
    perspectives.
-   **NewsOnMap** -- News shown on a map where the event happened.
-   **NewsPrep** -- AI-powered news test preparation with perspectives
    and debates.
-   **NewsTech** -- AI technology for newspapers and news companies.

## PersAnalytics

A personality testing platform for individuals, companies/organizations
and doctors. Includes most known personality tests.

## PingTales

A mobile text/WhatsApp/chat-based storytelling platform. Stories are
presented as chats similar to Netflix-style content. Users can both read
and create stories.

## Keekkoos

Media platform for children aged 3--12 featuring chat/image-based
stories, age-appropriate daily news and kids' contests.

## Silent Prayer

A religious app where people find places of worship on a map. Includes
an AI-powered prayer helper that explains the exact prayer, mantra,
deity/saint and method. Also supports digital pooja.

## Qoupled

A matrimony/matchmaker/dating app based on personality matching and
interests, fully powered by an AI assistant acting as a friend.

## WomSafe

A safety app for women, kids or anyone. The app checks in with users at
designated times. Users enter a safe PIN if everything is okay or an
unsafe PIN if they are in danger.

## Wowfy

-   **Wowfy Social / ChallengesOnMap** -- Challenge-based engagement for
    brands and celebrities.
-   **WowfyAtSchool/College** -- Student engagement through challenges.
-   **WowfyAtOffice** -- Office engagement through challenges.
-   **WowfyEngage** -- Temporary challenge system for hotels,
    restaurants, etc.

## Xortcut

Career guidance and hand-holding app that recommends suitable sectors,
clusters and careers based on personality and interests from Class 5
onwards.

## Xertify

AI-powered self-testing and certification platform for random topics.

## Xortlist

Career pipeline platform where people follow their dream jobs, complete
long-term challenges, earn rewards, internships, company visits and
interviews while companies identify talented candidates.

## Roice Ratings

Company providing proprietary rating systems and rankings such as hotel
compatibility, tourism viability index and language power rankings.

## Zuppdate

Live updates and interaction panels for bookings, events and services
from booking, during and after the event. Supports tokens for hospitals
and any queue-based system. Users can take queue tokens (next available
or scheduled), view live queue status, estimated calling time and
receive updates about breaks, emergencies and other changes.
